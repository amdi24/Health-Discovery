Heart Health

Heart health refers to the overall well-being of the heart and 
        cardiovascular system. Maintaining good heart health is essential for 
        preventing heart disease, which is a leading cause of death worldwide.
         Key factors that contribute to heart health include a balanced diet,
         regular physical activity, avoiding smoking, managing stress, 
         and monitoring blood pressure and cholesterol levels.
